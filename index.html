📋 How to Get Natal House Positions
Step 1 — Open AstroSage.com or DrikPanchang.com
Step 2 — Enter client DOB + birth time + birth city → generate Vedic birth chart
Step 3 — Note the Ascendant (Lagna) sign — this becomes House 1
Step 4 — Count forward from Lagna: next sign = H2, next = H3... up to H12
Step 5 — Find each planet in the chart — enter the house number it falls in
⚠️ Lal Kitab Rule
Lal Kitab ignores the zodiac signs (Rashis). Only the house number matters. Rahu and Ketu are always in houses exactly 7 apart — if Rahu is in H3, Ketu is in H9.
